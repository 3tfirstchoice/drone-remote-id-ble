# T05 - Mat nguon va khoi phuc

Yeu cau: sau khi cap lai nguon, thiet bi phat lai goi Remote ID trong < 3 s (lap 3 lan).

## Phuong phap
Firmware in dong `# first_tx ms=N` ngay khi goi BLE dau tien duoc phat; N tinh tu luc chip khoi dong (millis).
Script `firmware/tools/t05_measure.ps1` phat hien rut/cam cap qua cong COM8 va doc dong nay, 3 lan, moi lan rut cap 15 giay.
Phuong phap bam tay bang dong ho bi loai vi sai so phan xa cua nguoi.

## Ket qua

| Phien ban | BOOT_DELAY_MS | last_msecs | first_tx ms (3 lan) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Truoc khi sua | 2000 | 2000 | 2444, 2444, 2447 |
| Sau khi sua (patch #5) | 500 | 300 | 943, 943, 944 |

## Nhan xet
- first_tx ms khong gom thoi gian ROM va bootloader chay truoc khi millis() bat dau dem (uoc luong 0.3-0.6 s, khong do duoc).
- Truoc khi sua, tong thoi gian uoc luong ~2.8-3.0 s, sat nguong 3 s.
- Sau khi sua, tong thoi gian uoc luong ~1.3-1.6 s < 3 s: DAT.
- Nguyen nhan chinh truoc khi sua: thu vien chi phat khi millis() > 2000 (last_msecs), cong voi BOOT_DELAY_MS = 2000.
- Sau khi sua: T05_after.csv (30 giay) cho thay err = 0 va tx tang deu 6 goi/giay.
